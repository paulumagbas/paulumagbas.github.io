Process Documentation Sample

SOP: Daily Livestream Sales Reporting

Purpose: record each session accurately so daily, weekly, and monthly reports can be trusted.

  1. Confirm the session has ended and backend figures are final.
  2. Open the correct monthly tab in the tracker.
  3. Enter date, time slot, host, GMV, items sold, views, followers, hours.
  4. Check GMV per hour and the host's daily total and hours.
  5. Compare with backend figures and fix any mismatch.
  6. Confirm the weekly summary and ranking updated.
  7. Note unusual sessions and report unexplained figures to the supervisor.

Common issues: missing or duplicate row, host change mid-day, total mismatch.
